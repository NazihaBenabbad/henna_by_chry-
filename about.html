What is Henna?

Henna is a natural dye made from lawsonia plant. For thousands of years it has been a huge part of celebration in North Africa , the Middle East and Southeast Asia. It is used for celebrations, such as weddings and cultural tradtions.The reddish-brown stain afte use leaves behind beautiful designed stained on body parts. Henna is an emotion for many and holds a special part in every occasion.
