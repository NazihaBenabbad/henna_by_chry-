Project overview 

We built a website using this project to showcase Dewan Chowdhury’s small business, Henna by Chry, which provides professional henna services for any occasion. Our goal was to create a simple, user-friendly, and attractive website that offers potential customers quick access to information about my work, services, and booking options. Since this business is not only a class project but also a real operating business, we prioritized establishing a website design and brand identity that projects a professional, trustworthy, and functional image beyond just this assignment. The website features: a modern single-page layout, distinctive brand colors and fonts, an overview of the business and its services, a contact section, and a mobile-friendly design.

Challenges & What we Learned
1. Creating a unique Brand Identity That Represents Dewan’s Henna Business 
 This site represents Dewan’s business, as well as it creates a unique brand identity for it. This was accomplished by selecting careful color palettes to teach us how design selections impact professional perception, readability, and visual balance.  
2. Personal Privacy and Protection of Client Data  
One challenge we faced was ensuring the representation of how much personal information should be published. We decided to utilize only Dewan’s city as opposed to divulging the full address of the business to protect her privacy while also still giving the impression of credibility to clients. This had us thinking deeply about how clients may view our site once it is launched.  
3. Single-Page Layout Structure  
Finding that balance of simplicity and clarity with a single-page layout was a major challenge for us. We needed to determine which types of content are required so that users can quickly determine who we are, what we do, and how to contact us. 
 4. Creating a Real Business Site Versus a Fictional Project  
Because we will be using this site in the future, we paid attention to the spacing, typography, color-contrast, and the overall client experience of working with the site. This made us think beyond just completing an assignment and about the usability of our site over time.
What We Enjoyed
Creating a professional representation of Dewan’s business has been satisfying, and having a live website we can use gives us a strong sense of accomplishment.
